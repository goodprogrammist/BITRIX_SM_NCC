# Privacy Policy — Bitrix _NCC Cookie

Last updated: 2026-10-11

The extension does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal or usage data.

What the extension stores, only in your browser:
- settings (cookie name, icon colors) in the browser extension storage
  (`storage.sync`; the browser may synchronize it between your devices if you enabled browser sync);
- per-site choices (cookie domain, cookie name for the site) and marks of the cookies you turned on
  in the local extension storage.

What the extension does:
- sets and removes the _NCC cookie (BITRIX_SM_NCC or the name you configured) for the site you choose,
  and sets it again when the site deletes it while you keep it turned on;
- reads the address of the current tab only to know which site the cookie belongs to;
- reloads the current tab with the `?ncc=1` parameter when you press the corresponding button.

The extension makes no network requests of its own and contains no analytics or remote code.

Contact: https://github.com/goodprogrammist/BITRIX_SM_NCC/issues
